STOCKWISE

Never lose money to empty shelves or expired stock

An inventory alert tool built for small Pakistani retailers

Small shops track stock on paper or memory. StockWise tells them, in plain language, what to reorder and what to discount — before either becomes a loss.

The Problem

Every product a shop sells can fail two ways:

  • Stockout — it runs out, the customer buys elsewhere
  • Expiry — it sits too long, gets thrown away or discounted

Neither is visible until it's too late — no simple tool tells a small shop owner which items are at risk, this week, for either reason.

The Solution

StockWise takes a shop's stock numbers — uploaded as a spreadsheet or typed like a text message — and instantly flags every product as:

  • 🔴 Reorder now
  • 🟡 Discount now
  • 🟢 Fine, no action

Why WhatsApp

Small retailers in Pakistan already run their business over WhatsApp — ordering from suppliers, talking to customers.

Instead of asking them to learn new software, StockWise meets them where they already are: they text their stock, they get an answer back.

How It's Built

A Decision Tree model trained on stock level, sales speed, supplier lead time, and days-to-expiry decides the risk for every product.
